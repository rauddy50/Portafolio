Projects made

Replacing a patch panel with a stripped panel:

Faced with recurring telephony service failures caused by loose connections in an aging patch panel, I led the migration to a stripped panel under the critical constraint of zero service downtime. To overcome the challenge of a live cutover, we used UB2A bridging connectors to transfer active lines without interrupting ongoing calls, and UY2 connectors for inactive circuits. Professionally, this taught me that an organized environment and the right specialized tools prevent major complications and save valuable time. Spiritually, it reminded me that changing old habits feels difficult when we are in the middle of the transition, yet trusting the Lord’s guidance always leads to stronger, better outcomes even when we do not initially understand the process.

Reprogramming a Zetron Air Traffic Control Console:

Following the relocation of a Zetron communications console at Arroyo Barril International Airport, the unit lost its programming and failed to establish a data link during recovery due to an improperly wired serial programming cable. By diagnosing the physical interface against the manufacturer’s technical specifications, we rewired the cable back to its factory pinout, restoring communication and successfully reprogramming the console. Professionally, this taught me to always verify foundational physical connections and official documentation when troubleshooting critical systems. Spiritually, it reaffirmed that our Heavenly Father is always willing to guide and help us when we turn to Him, especially when we face moments where we do not know what to do.

Programming a Huawei CloudEngine S310 Switch:

While deploying new radio equipment for a remote communication station, our installation stalled because a team member was unable to properly save configuration changes on a Huawei CloudEngine S310 Series switch (V600R022C10). Although I had no prior experience with this specific model, I reviewed the official technical manual, mastered its configuration and save procedures, and fully programmed the switch within 30 minutes—accelerating the entire deployment. Professionally, this reinforced the value of technical documentation and adaptability when working with unfamiliar hardware. Spiritually, it reminded me that our Heavenly Father gives us talents we often do not discover until we step up to use them, helping me recognize my gift for rapid learning.
