Replacing a patch panel with a stripped panel:

Faced with recurring telephony service failures caused by loose connections in an aging patch panel, I led the migration to a stripped panel under the critical constraint of zero service downtime. To overcome the challenge of a live cutover, we used UB2A bridging connectors to transfer active lines without interrupting ongoing calls, and UY2 connectors for inactive circuits. Professionally, this taught me that an organized environment and the right specialized tools prevent major complications and save valuable time. Spiritually, it reminded me that changing old habits feels difficult when we are in the middle of the transition, yet trusting the Lord’s guidance always leads to stronger, better outcomes even when we do not initially understand the process.
